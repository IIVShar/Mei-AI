How to Setup & Use the Gemini Voice API

Learn how OpenAudio Studio connects to Google's Gemini TTS model.

1

Get a Free Google AI Studio Key

Visit aistudio.google.com, log in with your Google account, click "Get API Key" and copy your key.

2

Paste key into Voice API Studio

Switch to the "Gemini Voice API Studio" tab and paste your key into the Google Gemini API Key input box.

3

How the App Processes API Audio

When you click Synthesize, the app posts your text to gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts requesting response modality ["AUDIO"]. The returned 16-bit PCM audio stream is automatically decoded in your browser into a playable WAV blob.
